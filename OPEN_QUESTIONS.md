## OQ-002  debt metric formula does not match its own governing decision
Raised: 2026-08-21   Task: Sprint 3, deterministic compiler (corpus/05 metric registry, `debt`)
Blocked by: conflict within the corpus (corpus/05 vs corpus/00 D-035, same metric)
What I need: corpus/05's `debt` row gives the formula as `gl_class(liability.debt_*)`, a prefix match. That pattern matches `liability.debt_term`, `debt_working_capital`, `debt_current_maturity` and `debt_related_party`, but NOT `liability.bill_discounting` (different prefix). D-035's own resolution text (corpus/00 section 2b) says debt "includes term loans, cash credit and overdraft, current maturities, bill discounting (per D-036) and unsecured related-party loans" -- explicitly including bill discounting. The formula and the decision it is governed by disagree on the same metric.
Options I can see: (a) the formula should be `gl_class(liability.debt_*|liability.bill_discounting)` and the corpus's formula string is the stale artifact; (b) D-035's resolution prose is the aspirational/prose description and the formula's narrower pattern is the deliberately-scoped literal definition (e.g. bill discounting is meant to surface only via the separate `liability.bill_discounting` judgement class review, never folded into the automated `debt` roll-up); (c) some other resolution.
What I will NOT do: silently widen or narrow the formula pattern to match what I think D-035 "meant."
Downstream blocked: `src/semantic/compiler.py`'s leaf resolver for the `debt` metric implements the formula exactly as written in corpus/05 (`liability.debt_*`, excluding bill discounting) -- so `debt` and everything derived from it (`net_debt`) will run 2-5 percentage points light on any company that carries bill-discounted receivables, until this is resolved.

**Resolved 2026-08-24 by Bhavya Singhal, founder.** D-035's resolved text is authoritative; the formula was the stale artifact. corpus/05's `debt` row updated to `gl_class(liability.debt_*|liability.bill_discounting)`, `config/metrics/debt.yml` regenerated via `scripts/gen_metric_contracts.py`. `match_gl_classes` (`src/semantic/formula.py`) already supports `|`-separated exact classes mixed with a wildcard within one `gl_class(...)` argument, so no compiler code changed.

## OQ-004  Eight cash flow leaf metrics have no formula or canonical class anywhere in the corpus
Raised: 2026-08-21   Task: Sprint 5, indirect cash flow statement (corpus/08 section 6, corpus/03 section 4)
Blocked by: corpus gap
What I need: corpus/03 section 4 gives exact formulas for `operating_cash_flow`, `investing_cash_flow` and `financing_cash_flow` in terms of named components -- but eight of those components (`taxes_paid`, `asset_disposals`, `investment_movements`, `debt_drawn`, `debt_repaid`, `equity_raised`, `dividends`, `interest_paid`) have no metric row in corpus/05, no gl_class formula, and no canonical class in corpus/06's taxonomy anywhere. This is not the same shape as OQ-001/OQ-003 (an acknowledged-but-unquantified threshold) -- these are formula inputs with no definition at all, not even a placeholder. By contrast `capex`, `pbt`, `da`, `interest_expense`, `other_income` and `working_capital_change` (the other components of the same three formulas) are all fully specified with literal `gl_class(...)` or `delta gl_class(...)` expressions, which is why I'm confident the other eight are a real gap and not something I'm missing.
A secondary, smaller inconsistency in the same area: corpus/03 section 4 cites "D-037. Interest paid versus interest expense differ where interest is accrued but unpaid" as the decision governing that distinction, but D-037's actual resolved text (corpus/00, section E) is "Capex measured as additions to gross block plus capital WIP movement, not cash paid" -- a different subject entirely. Either the citation is stale or a decision id is missing.
Options I can see: (a) these eight are meant to be computed from balance sheet deltas the same way `capex` is (e.g. `debt_drawn`/`debt_repaid` as split signs of the net delta across the debt classes in D-035's list, `investment_movements` as delta of `asset.investment`, `equity_raised` as delta of `equity.share_capital`) -- plausible but nowhere stated, and gross drawn/repaid specifically cannot be recovered from a net period-end balance if both a draw and a repayment happened in the same month; (b) they require a transactional feed this system does not ingest (bank statement doesn't tag purpose, no loan register, no dividend/tax-payment source) and are genuinely out of P0 despite `p0_statement: yes` on `investing_cash_flow`/`financing_cash_flow` in corpus/05; (c) some other resolution.
What I will NOT do: derive `debt_drawn`/`debt_repaid`/`equity_raised`/`dividends`/`interest_paid`/`asset_disposals`/`investment_movements`/`taxes_paid` from balance sheet deltas on my own judgement and present the result as the defined metric.
Downstream blocked: `src/reports/cashflow.py` computes `working_capital_change` in full (fully specified, itemised by receivables/inventory/payables/other operating per corpus/08 section 6) and the four fully-specified components of `operating_cash_flow` (`pbt`, `da`, `interest_expense`, `other_income`), but reports `operating_cash_flow` itself, `investing_cash_flow`, `financing_cash_flow`, `net_cash_movement` and `closing_cash` as not configured. Per corpus/08 section 5's own hard gate ("closing_cash must equal balance sheet cash exactly, or neither displays"), the cash flow statement therefore does not display in the pack or the statements screen until this resolves -- it is reported as a known gap in every pack's section 9, not hidden.

**Partially resolved 2026-08-24 by Bhavya Singhal, founder.** Decision: derive what's derivable from balance-sheet deltas, using classes the taxonomy already has, and explicitly leave the rest undefined rather than guess. Two of the eight terms now have a formula: `investment_movements` (delta of `asset.investment`) and `equity_raised` (delta of `equity.share_capital`), both implemented in `src/reports/cashflow.py` and documented in corpus/03 section 4. The remaining six (`taxes_paid`, `asset_disposals`, `debt_drawn`, `debt_repaid`, `dividends`, `interest_paid`) stay undefined by explicit decision: `debt_drawn`/`debt_repaid` can't be split from `debt`'s single net period-end delta (a draw and a repayment in the same month collapse to one number, same structural problem as `asset_disposals` against `fixed_asset_gross`'s net-only movement), and `taxes_paid`/`dividends`/`interest_paid` have no payable-side canonical class (no `liability.tax_payable`, `liability.dividend_payable` or `liability.interest_payable` in the taxonomy) to derive an accrual adjustment from. `operating_cash_flow`, `investing_cash_flow`, `financing_cash_flow` and `closing_cash` therefore still do not reconcile and the cash flow statement still does not display -- `reconciles=False` is unchanged, only the disclosed detail improved. Still open: whether to add the missing payable-side canonical classes (a taxonomy change, not a corpus-reading exercise) or declare the remaining six formally out of P0 scope.

## OQ-007  A balance sheet cannot balance while any account sits in suspense, but D-053 says small unmapped value is fine
Raised: 2026-08-23   Task: eval suite, statements tie-out (corpus/08 section 5, corpus/06 section 3.6, D-053)
Blocked by: conflict within the corpus (D-053 / corpus/06 section 3.6 vs CLAUDE.md invariant 9 and corpus/08 section 5)
What I need: the trial balance sums to exactly zero across every class (D-051, zero tolerance). So for any period, sum(balance sheet classes) + sum(P&L classes) + sum(suspense.unmapped) = 0. `src/reports/balance_sheet.compute_balance_sheet` derives retained earnings as the negative of the cumulative P&L total, which is the correct closing entry, and excludes `suspense.unmapped` from both sides because corpus/06's taxonomy gives that class `statement_section: memo`, not `bs`. The arithmetic consequence is exact and unavoidable: assets minus liabilities-and-equity equals the signed suspense balance. **Any company with a single unmapped account carrying a non-zero balance can therefore never display a balance sheet**, because CLAUDE.md invariant 9 and corpus/08 section 5 forbid displaying one that does not balance.
That collides with D-053, which is resolved and says unmapped value is "silent below 0.5%" of period value and only blocks above 2%. D-053 clearly contemplates a company operating normally with a small unmapped tail; corpus/06 section 3.6 describes exactly that ("the long tail sits in a suspense class, visible and quantified in rupees, until someone cares"). Both statements cannot hold at once while suspense is a memo class: below 0.5% the corpus says carry on, and invariant 9 says the balance sheet is withheld.
Options I can see: (a) `suspense.unmapped` should appear as its own line on the balance sheet rather than as a memo, so the sheet balances with the unclassified amount visible on its face -- this is the only option that satisfies D-053, corpus/06 section 3.6 and invariant 9 simultaneously, but it contradicts the `statement_section: memo` that corpus/06's own taxonomy assigns it; (b) the balance sheet is genuinely meant to be withheld until the mapping is 100% complete by value, and D-053's silent/badge bands govern only metric serving and pack generation, never the balance sheet -- in which case corpus/02 section 8's "98 percent of period value mapped before statements unlock" is the statement that needs rewording, because 98% is not enough to display a balance sheet and never can be; (c) retained earnings should be derived from the trial balance including suspense rather than from mapped P&L classes only, which would balance the sheet by treating unclassified value as a P&L residual -- an accounting treatment no corpus file states; (d) some other resolution.
What I will NOT do: move `suspense.unmapped` onto the balance sheet, or fold it into retained earnings, on my own judgement. Where an unclassified balance is presented is a financial-statement classification decision, which CLAUDE.md section 3.1 forbids me from inventing, and either choice changes a published balance sheet.
Downstream blocked: both synthetic reference companies carry a deliberately unmappable long tail (corpus/11 section 2.2 seeds it as defect 10), so neither balance sheet balances and neither displays -- correct behaviour today, and the system reports the gap rather than hiding it. `tests/eval/test_statements_tie_manufacturer.py` and `tests/eval/test_statements_tie_consumer.py` now assert the provable property instead: that the imbalance equals the signed suspense balance exactly, so the gap is fully explained and nothing is unaccounted for. They should be changed back to asserting `bs.balances` the moment this is resolved. The consumer company is additionally blocked by OQ-006.

**Resolved 2026-08-24 by Bhavya Singhal, founder.** Option (a): `suspense.unmapped` gets its own balance sheet line rather than being excluded from both sides. corpus/06a's Canonical Classes tab updated: `statement_section` for `suspense.unmapped` changed MEMO -> BS (statement_line: unclassified); `config/taxonomy.yml` regenerated. `src/reports/statement_lines.py`'s `BALANCE_SHEET_LINES` gained `"suspense.unmapped": ("unclassified", "Unclassified (suspense)", REVENUE_SIGN)` -- the sign is the one that makes `total_assets == total_liabilities_and_equity` hold by construction (derived from the trial balance's own zero-tolerance identity, D-051), not a copy of the liability presentation convention. `src/reports/balance_sheet.py`'s `compute_balance_sheet` no longer `continue`s past `suspense.unmapped` before adding it to a group; `total_liabilities_and_equity` now includes the new `unclassified` group total. `unmapped_value_inr` (D-053's badge/block input) is still tracked independently, unaffected. Net effect: the balance sheet now balances whenever the trial balance does (always, D-051), regardless of how much sits in suspense, with the unmapped amount visible in rupees on the face of the sheet rather than causing it to be withheld. Both eval tests updated accordingly: `test_statements_tie_consumer.py` now asserts `bs.balances is True` outright (OQ-006 leaves nothing in that company's suspense at all, and it has no genuine trial-balance-breaking defect). `test_statements_tie_manufacturer.py` still asserts `bs.balances is False`, but for a narrower reason than before -- defect #4 (corpus/11 section 2.2) deliberately posts one genuinely unbalanced voucher, independent of suspense, so that company's trial balance itself is nonzero regardless of this fix; its identity assertion is updated to drop the now-cancelled `- suspense_signed` term. Neither test change has been run against a live Postgres in this session -- verify with `pytest tests/eval -k statements_tie` before relying on it.
