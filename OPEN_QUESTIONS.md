## OQ-004  Eight cash flow leaf metrics have no formula or canonical class anywhere in the corpus

## OQ-007  A balance sheet cannot balance while any account sits in suspense, but D-053 says small unmapped value is fine
Raised: 2026-08-23   Task: eval suite, statements tie-out (corpus/08 section 5, corpus/06 section 3.6, D-053)
Blocked by: conflict within the corpus (D-053 / corpus/06 section 3.6 vs CLAUDE.md invariant 9 and corpus/08 section 5)
What I need: the trial balance sums to exactly zero across every class (D-051, zero tolerance). So for any period, sum(balance sheet classes) + sum(P&L classes) + sum(suspense.unmapped) = 0. `src/reports/balance_sheet.compute_balance_sheet` derives retained earnings as the negative of the cumulative P&L total, which is the correct closing entry, and excludes `suspense.unmapped` from both sides because corpus/06's taxonomy gives that class `statement_section: memo`, not `bs`. The arithmetic consequence is exact and unavoidable: assets minus liabilities-and-equity equals the signed suspense balance. **Any company with a single unmapped account carrying a non-zero balance can therefore never display a balance sheet**, because CLAUDE.md invariant 9 and corpus/08 section 5 forbid displaying one that does not balance.
That collides with D-053, which is resolved and says unmapped value is "silent below 0.5%" of period value and only blocks above 2%. D-053 clearly contemplates a company operating normally with a small unmapped tail; corpus/06 section 3.6 describes exactly that ("the long tail sits in a suspense class, visible and quantified in rupees, until someone cares"). Both statements cannot hold at once while suspense is a memo class: below 0.5% the corpus says carry on, and invariant 9 says the balance sheet is withheld.
Options I can see: (a) `suspense.unmapped` should appear as its own line on the balance sheet rather than as a memo, so the sheet balances with the unclassified amount visible on its face -- this is the only option that satisfies D-053, corpus/06 section 3.6 and invariant 9 simultaneously, but it contradicts the `statement_section: memo` that corpus/06's own taxonomy assigns it; (b) the balance sheet is genuinely meant to be withheld until the mapping is 100% complete by value, and D-053's silent/badge bands govern only metric serving and pack generation, never the balance sheet -- in which case corpus/02 section 8's "98 percent of period value mapped before statements unlock" is the statement that needs rewording, because 98% is not enough to display a balance sheet and never can be; (c) retained earnings should be derived from the trial balance including suspense rather than from mapped P&L classes only, which would balance the sheet by treating unclassified value as a P&L residual -- an accounting treatment no corpus file states; (d) some other resolution.
What I will NOT do: move `suspense.unmapped` onto the balance sheet, or fold it into retained earnings, on my own judgement. Where an unclassified balance is presented is a financial-statement classification decision, which CLAUDE.md section 3.1 forbids me from inventing, and either choice changes a published balance sheet.
Downstream blocked: both synthetic reference companies carry a deliberately unmappable long tail (corpus/11 section 2.2 seeds it as defect 10), so neither balance sheet balances and neither displays -- correct behaviour today, and the system reports the gap rather than hiding it. `tests/eval/test_statements_tie_manufacturer.py` and `tests/eval/test_statements_tie_consumer.py` now assert the provable property instead: that the imbalance equals the signed suspense balance exactly, so the gap is fully explained and nothing is unaccounted for. They should be changed back to asserting `bs.balances` the moment this is resolved. The consumer company is additionally blocked by OQ-006.
