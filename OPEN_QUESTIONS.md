## OQ-002  debt metric formula does not match its own governing decision
Raised: 2026-08-21   Task: Sprint 3, deterministic compiler (corpus/05 metric registry, `debt`)
Blocked by: conflict within the corpus (corpus/05 vs corpus/00 D-035, same metric)
What I need: corpus/05's `debt` row gives the formula as `gl_class(liability.debt_*)`, a prefix match. That pattern matches `liability.debt_term`, `debt_working_capital`, `debt_current_maturity` and `debt_related_party`, but NOT `liability.bill_discounting` (different prefix). D-035's own resolution text (corpus/00 section 2b) says debt "includes term loans, cash credit and overdraft, current maturities, bill discounting (per D-036) and unsecured related-party loans" -- explicitly including bill discounting. The formula and the decision it is governed by disagree on the same metric.
Options I can see: (a) the formula should be `gl_class(liability.debt_*|liability.bill_discounting)` and the corpus's formula string is the stale artifact; (b) D-035's resolution prose is the aspirational/prose description and the formula's narrower pattern is the deliberately-scoped literal definition (e.g. bill discounting is meant to surface only via the separate `liability.bill_discounting` judgement class review, never folded into the automated `debt` roll-up); (c) some other resolution.
What I will NOT do: silently widen or narrow the formula pattern to match what I think D-035 "meant."
Downstream blocked: `src/semantic/compiler.py`'s leaf resolver for the `debt` metric implements the formula exactly as written in corpus/05 (`liability.debt_*`, excluding bill discounting) -- so `debt` and everything derived from it (`net_debt`) will run 2-5 percentage points light on any company that carries bill-discounted receivables, until this is resolved.

## OQ-004  Eight cash flow leaf metrics have no formula or canonical class anywhere in the corpus
Raised: 2026-08-21   Task: Sprint 5, indirect cash flow statement (corpus/08 section 6, corpus/03 section 4)
Blocked by: corpus gap
What I need: corpus/03 section 4 gives exact formulas for `operating_cash_flow`, `investing_cash_flow` and `financing_cash_flow` in terms of named components -- but eight of those components (`taxes_paid`, `asset_disposals`, `investment_movements`, `debt_drawn`, `debt_repaid`, `equity_raised`, `dividends`, `interest_paid`) have no metric row in corpus/05, no gl_class formula, and no canonical class in corpus/06's taxonomy anywhere. This is not the same shape as OQ-001/OQ-003 (an acknowledged-but-unquantified threshold) -- these are formula inputs with no definition at all, not even a placeholder. By contrast `capex`, `pbt`, `da`, `interest_expense`, `other_income` and `working_capital_change` (the other components of the same three formulas) are all fully specified with literal `gl_class(...)` or `delta gl_class(...)` expressions, which is why I'm confident the other eight are a real gap and not something I'm missing.
A secondary, smaller inconsistency in the same area: corpus/03 section 4 cites "D-037. Interest paid versus interest expense differ where interest is accrued but unpaid" as the decision governing that distinction, but D-037's actual resolved text (corpus/00, section E) is "Capex measured as additions to gross block plus capital WIP movement, not cash paid" -- a different subject entirely. Either the citation is stale or a decision id is missing.
Options I can see: (a) these eight are meant to be computed from balance sheet deltas the same way `capex` is (e.g. `debt_drawn`/`debt_repaid` as split signs of the net delta across the debt classes in D-035's list, `investment_movements` as delta of `asset.investment`, `equity_raised` as delta of `equity.share_capital`) -- plausible but nowhere stated, and gross drawn/repaid specifically cannot be recovered from a net period-end balance if both a draw and a repayment happened in the same month; (b) they require a transactional feed this system does not ingest (bank statement doesn't tag purpose, no loan register, no dividend/tax-payment source) and are genuinely out of P0 despite `p0_statement: yes` on `investing_cash_flow`/`financing_cash_flow` in corpus/05; (c) some other resolution.
What I will NOT do: derive `debt_drawn`/`debt_repaid`/`equity_raised`/`dividends`/`interest_paid`/`asset_disposals`/`investment_movements`/`taxes_paid` from balance sheet deltas on my own judgement and present the result as the defined metric.
Downstream blocked: `src/reports/cashflow.py` computes `working_capital_change` in full (fully specified, itemised by receivables/inventory/payables/other operating per corpus/08 section 6) and the four fully-specified components of `operating_cash_flow` (`pbt`, `da`, `interest_expense`, `other_income`), but reports `operating_cash_flow` itself, `investing_cash_flow`, `financing_cash_flow`, `net_cash_movement` and `closing_cash` as not configured. Per corpus/08 section 5's own hard gate ("closing_cash must equal balance sheet cash exactly, or neither displays"), the cash flow statement therefore does not display in the pack or the statements screen until this resolves -- it is reported as a known gap in every pack's section 9, not hidden.
